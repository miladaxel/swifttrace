Check payment claims with your bank

A receipt, MT103 text, screenshot, or record on this site is not proof that funds reached the beneficiary account.

Confirm receipt before releasing goods or services

Ask the receiving bank to confirm whether funds were credited. The sending bank can investigate a delayed payment using its own records.

Information to compare

  • Check that the UETR or payment reference matches the document you were given.
  • Compare the amount, currency, payment date, and beneficiary details with your own records.
  • Ask your bank about missing information or mismatches. A well-formatted document can still be inaccurate or altered.

Our record search shows only information entered by an administrator. The MT103 text tool extracts fields but does not determine whether a document is genuine.
