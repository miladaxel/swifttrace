Troubleshooting Intelligence

Why cross-border wires stall & how Swift helps

90% of cross-border payment delays happen at intermediary correspondent banks due to simple formatting or screening issues.

Missing Purpose-of-Payment Codes

Jurisdictions like the UAE, India, and China require specific regulatory purpose codes in Field 70 (or pacs.008 equivalent) for Straight-Through Processing (STP). Without them, the wire gets held for manual review.

Unexpected Fee Deductions (SHA/BEN)

When wires route through multiple correspondent banks, each intermediary may deduct fees from the principal amount under SHA or BEN charge codes. Swifttrace helps estimate landed deductions before dispatch.

False-Positive Sanctions Holds

Automated bank filters frequently halt payments when beneficiary names or vessel details partially match sanctioned entities. Screening with fuzzy matching before transmission reduces compliance freezes.

Payment Document Caution & Scam Protection

Fraudulent actors frequently fabricate MT103 receipts, bills of lading, or spoof tracking domains to deceive sellers into releasing goods before settlement. A published record or confirmation slip is not proof of receipt until funds settle into your bank account.
