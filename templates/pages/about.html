About this project

An independent prototype for searching administrator-published payment records.

Administrators enter payment identifiers, amounts, parties, recorded status, source information, and optional supporting files. A record becomes searchable only after publication.

Users can look up a published record by UETR or by payment reference together with its date and amount. The result shows the recorded status and when that status was observed. This project does not query a bank or the SWIFT network.

The IBAN tool checks format and check digits. The MT103 tool extracts selected fields from pasted text. Corridor pages and name matching use illustrative data.

Swifttrace is an independent prototype and is not affiliated with S.W.I.F.T. SC. It does not move money or verify that a transfer settled. Confirm payment status with your bank.
