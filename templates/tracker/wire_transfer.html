How to find your payment record

The information you enter must match a record that an administrator has published here.

  1. 1

    Get the payment details

    Ask the sender or sending bank for the payment confirmation. Look for the UETR, reference, date, amount and currency.

  2. 2

    Search this portal

    Enter the UETR if available. With only a reference, also enter the date and amount. Currency can narrow an ambiguous result.

  3. 3

    Read the recorded status

    Check when the status was observed and which stages were entered. Ask your bank for an official update or confirmation of receipt.

Where is the UETR?

A UETR is a 36-character payment identifier in five groups. On an MT103 it may appear in field 121 of the header; some bank confirmations label it “UETR” or “gpi reference”. If it is missing, ask the sending bank.

Using a payment reference

The reference may appear as “transaction reference” or field 20 on an MT103. References can be reused, so this portal also requires the payment date and amount. If several records match, add the currency or obtain the UETR.

Understanding a recorded status

These labels describe what the administrator recorded, as of the date shown on the result. “Held” is a portal category.

PDNG · Pending
The record is awaiting a later status.
ACSP · In process
The recorded status says settlement is in process.
ACCC · Completed / credited
The source reported completion or credit at the time recorded.
RJCT · Rejected / returned
The source reported a rejection or return.
HOLD · Held / review
The administrator marked the record for a hold or review.

Frequently asked questions

Can I search without a UETR?

Yes. Enter the payment reference, date and amount. Add currency if the search returns more than one matching record.

Why was no document found?

The details may differ from the published record, or the administrator may not have published one. Check the confirmation and ask the team that provided this portal about its records. A missing result does not prove the payment failed.

Does a result confirm that money arrived?

No. This page displays information entered by an administrator. Contact the receiving bank for confirmation of receipt and the sending bank for an official payment investigation.

Can I upload a PDF to search?

No. Public search uses the fields you enter above. The MT103 text tool extracts selected fields from pasted text; it does not authenticate a document.
